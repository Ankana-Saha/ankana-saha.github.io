This year Sinn und Bedeutung (SuB 29) was held on September 16–20, 2024 in Noto, Sicily, with several Harvard semanticists participating. My advisor, Gennaro Chierchia gave an invited talk on “At the frontier of quantification: correlatives, movement and scope.” PhD student Natasha Thalluri presented a poster on “A return to a functional dependency approach to correlatives.”, and I gave a talk on “Demonstratives and alternatives.”

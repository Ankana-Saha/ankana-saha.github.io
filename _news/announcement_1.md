I'm excited to share that the paper “The or That? Evaluating language models’ sensitivity to discourse structure in anaphora” by Jennifer Hu (Johns Hopkins University), Kate Davidson and myself has been accepted to the Pragmatic Reasoning in Language Models Workshop at the Conference on Language Modeling (COLM 2025), which will take place in Montreal on October 10, 2025.
